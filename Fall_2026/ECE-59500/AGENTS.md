# ECE 69500 Assignment Guidelines

These instructions apply when helping with any assignment in this course.

## Deliverable Expectations

- Formatting is flexible, but presentation quality matters.
- Prefer first person in all assignment reports. Use "I" for actions Ricardo performed and preserve that voice when revising his prose.
- In LaTeX prose, keep each complete sentence on one physical source line. Insert prose line breaks only after sentence-ending periods; never hard-wrap a sentence based on line length.
- The report must demonstrate that each required activity was completed successfully; include appropriate evidence rather than merely claiming success.
- Explain what needs to be communicated and why it matters.
- Write for the report's audience at an appropriate level of depth.
- Include enough background to understand the work, but avoid background that is excessive or irrelevant.
- Do not fabricate commands, outputs, screenshots, measurements, or successful results. Run the work when possible and use the actual evidence Ricardo obtains.

## Grading Priorities

- Show whether the activity was completed successfully.
- Demonstrate understanding of why and how it worked, rather than presenting a solution reached by trial and error without explanation.
- Present the methodology and results clearly and correctly.
- Treat every points box in an assignment as an explicit report requirement and use it to determine what must be included.
- Always complete and document every extra-credit activity; treat extra credit as a required part of Ricardo's deliverable.
- Incorporate instructor feedback in later reports. Initial mistakes may receive feedback without a deduction, but repeating the same issue can lose points.
- Prioritize learning and improvement over superficial perfection, and identify questions that should be taken to the instructor when requirements or results remain unclear.
